Demo payload. The shape follows the usual webhook pattern: a signed body, a timestamp against replays, and a key the receiving app can use to refuse a second copy.

When Accounting is down, orders wait. They do not vanish.

A no-code chain usually tries once and moves on. Bothways keeps the event, backs off, and tries again on a schedule that gives the other app room to recover instead of flooding it.

The retry schedule

  1. Try 1at once
  2. Try 2+15 s
  3. Try 3+1 min
  4. Try 4+4 min
  5. Try 5+15 min
  6. Try 6+1 h
  7. Try 7+4 h

The app is down or too slow to answer (503, timeout): retried on this schedule, then held for a person with a Replay button.

The app asks us to slow down (429): we wait exactly as long as it says, then carry on.

The app says the data is wrong (422, 400): not retried, because the same data would fail again. A person is asked, with the reason.

The same order, delivered twice.

Online store delivered the same order.paid event for #1042 again at 09:41:09. Webhook senders promise "at least once", not "exactly once", so this happens on ordinary days.

The key store:evt_7Hq2Lx was already saved, so the second copy was skipped without being read. Accounting has one invoice for order #1042.

Describe your two apps

Two apps at a time, and the records between them.

Each job is one pair of apps and a short list of what should move between them. Keeping it that narrow is what makes the quote fixed and the log readable.

  • Form tool CRM

    New submissions become contacts. Matched by email, so a returning visitor is updated, not added twice.

  • Online store Accounting

    Paid orders become invoices with the right tax code. Refunds become credit notes against the same invoice.

  • Helpdesk Team chat

    Urgent tickets post to the right channel. Replies thread under the first post instead of starting new ones.

  • Booking app Calendar

    Each booking keeps one calendar event through moves and cancellations.

  • Payments Spreadsheet

    Each payout lands as one row with fees split out, ready for the monthly close.

  • Your two apps

    If one side can send webhooks or be read through an API, and the other can be written to, the quote says how. If it cannot be done well, the quote says that too.

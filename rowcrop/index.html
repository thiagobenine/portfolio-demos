Sample first. Then the full run.

Nothing runs at scale until you have seen your own table. Every job starts with a sample of about 30 rows, so you can check the column names, formats and odd cases before we read every page.

How a job runs, illustrative timeline
WhenStepWhat happensWho
Mon 09:12RequestYou send a website and the columns you need, in plain words: price as shown, before discount, in stock, yes or no.You
Mon 15:40SampleWe send about 30 real rows with every column you named, plus a note on anything the pages do not show.Rowcrop
Tue 10:05ConfirmYou approve the columns or change them. Rating becomes rating and review count. We send a new sample if anything changed.You
Tue 22:00Full runEvery matching page is read. Duplicates are removed by the key you agreed, values are typed and normalized, and each row keeps its source URL.Rowcrop
Wed 06:00DeliveryThe table lands where you work. On a schedule, every later run updates the same place and flags what changed.Rowcrop

Times are illustrative. How long a job takes depends on the site and the number of pages.

When a store redesigns, you hear it from us first.

Sites change without warning. When a source stops matching, we hold the run instead of shipping broken rows, tell you, and update how we read that site.

Public pages only. No logins, ever.

We collect what anyone can see without an account, and we respect each site's terms and robots rules. If a site cannot be collected that way, we tell you before any work starts.

What Rowcrop collects and never collects
We collectWe never collect
Prices, stock and ratings shown on public product pagesAnything behind a login, an account or a paywall
Product names, specs, categories and image URLsPersonal data about private individuals
Business listings and companies' public contact pagesPages a site's terms or robots rules put off limits
Your own store's pages, to compare like with likeContent that needs a CAPTCHA solved to be seen

PIXPaid in seconds, or it expires

PIX is Brazil's instant payment system, run by the central bank. You create a charge and show its QR code, or its copy-and-paste code on desktop. The customer approves it in their bank app and the money moves in seconds, at any hour.

A PIX charge can't be paid after it expires, so every charge ends in a clear yes or no.

24/7, weekends and holidays included

BoletoA bill with a due date

A boleto is a payment slip with a barcode and a 47-digit typeable line. People without a credit card, and many companies, pay it in a banking app, at an ATM or at a lottery shop. The bank network confirms the payment, usually the next business day, and then you get the webhook.

  1. Mon 5 OctSlip issued with the order charge.created
  2. Wed 7 OctMariana pays it in her bank app
  3. Thu 8 OctBank network confirms charge.paid
  4. Fri 9 OctDue date. Unpaid slips expire after the grace days you set

CardsThe price tag says 10x

Brazilian shoppers expect to split a card purchase into monthly installments, called parcelado, and stores print the installment bigger than the price. You decide how many installments to offer and who pays for them: you (sem juros, interest-free for the customer) or the customer, with interest.

Then pick how the money reaches you: one installment each month as the card issuer settles it, or everything two days after the sale, minus an anticipation fee.

    Verify the signature, then fulfil

    webhooks.js
    import { verifyWebhook } from "@cobogo/node";

app.post("/hooks/cobogo",
  express.raw({ type: "application/json" }),
  async (req, res) => {
    // Throws on a bad HMAC or a timestamp older than 5 min
    const event = verifyWebhook(
      req.body,
      req.header("Cobogo-Signature"),
      process.env.COBOGO_WEBHOOK_SECRET
    );
    if (event.type === "charge.paid") {
      // Retries reuse event.id: a second markPaid is a no-op
      await orders.markPaid(event.data.metadata.order_id, {
        eventId: event.id,
      });
    }
    res.sendStatus(200);
  }
);

    One handler for all three lines. PIX, boleto and cards all arrive as charge.paid with the same payload shape, so this is the only fulfilment code you write.

    Down for an hour? List events from the API and replay any of them once you are back up. The same event id keeps markPaid safe.

    Before you launch in Brazil

    The questions foreign teams ask first. Something else? Write to hello@cobogo.example.

    Do we need a company or bank account in Brazil?

    No. Cobogó collects in Brazil as your local processor, converts to USD or EUR and pays out to your existing bank account abroad.

    Why do you ask for the customer's CPF?

    The CPF is the Brazilian individual taxpayer number (companies use a CNPJ). Boletos require it and banks use it in PIX and fraud checks. Send it in customer.tax_id; we check the format and check digits before creating the charge.

    What happens when a PIX code expires?

    The QR code stops working in every bank app and you receive charge.expired. Create a new charge with a new idempotency key if the customer comes back.

    Can a boleto be paid after its due date?

    Only within the grace period you set, up to 30 days, with the late fee and interest you configure. After that, banks refuse it and you receive charge.expired.

    How do refunds work for each method?

    PIX refunds go back to the payer's account as a PIX. Card refunds return to the card, and pending installments are cancelled. Boleto refunds need the customer's bank details, which we collect through a hosted page.

    What if our webhook endpoint is down?

    We retry with exponential backoff for 72 hours, keeping the same event id. You can also list events from the API and replay any of them once you are back up.

Three landing pages, each with its own visual world.

Designed and coded by Thiago as portfolio demos: a subscription coffee roaster, a time-tracking tool for studios and a payments API for selling to Brazil. Every page is responsive and hand-built in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The brands are fictional.

  1. Rest Days

    Coffee subscription

    A roaster's packing bench, stamped in one violet ink. Pick your brew method and delivery rhythm and the calendar re-stamps itself with roast days, rest days and the best days to brew.

  2. Hourcard

    Time tracking for studios

    A studio desk on Friday: a time card, a client report and an invoice copy on a cutting mat. The week closes on screen, every entry ticks to billed and the invoice slides out with its totals.

    Open the page

  3. Cobogó

    Payments API for Brazil

    Built on the modernist tile walls of Brasília. A PIX charge is paid on screen, its QR squares flip to green, a signed webhook reaches the store and the order is marked paid. The QR code only decodes to a demo notice.

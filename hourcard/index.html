Questions studios ask before switching.

What if someone forgets to start the timer?

They add the entry by hand afterwards, or drag a block on the day view. Hand-written entries are marked, so you can always tell what was timed and what was typed.

What happens when a timer runs all night?

Hourcard notices when a computer goes idle and asks what to keep when the person is back. Any entry longer than ten hours is flagged for review before Friday's reports are drafted.

Can clients see internal notes or non-billable time?

No. Anything marked internal stays in the studio view. The client report and the invoice only show billable work, under the task names you choose.

Do we have to send invoices through Hourcard?

No. You can send reports only and export the hours as CSV to the invoicing tool you already use.

Can we bring clients and rates over from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Upload a CSV with client, project and rate columns. Hourcard shows a preview of every row before anything is imported.

What happens when the trial ends?

You choose a plan, or the account becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted, and you can export everything at any time.

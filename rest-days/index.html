    The short answers to what people ask before they start.

    When is the cutoff for the next roast?

    Sunday at 11 pm Eastern. Anything you order or change after that goes into the following Monday's roast.

    When am I charged?

    At the Sunday 11 pm cutoff before each roast, once your bag is locked into Monday's batch. Skip or pause before the cutoff and nothing is charged for that week.

    Why does fresh coffee need to rest?

    Freshly roasted beans keep releasing carbon dioxide for days. Brewed too soon, that gas pushes water away from the grounds and the cup tastes sharp and thin. Filter needs about 5 days and espresso about 10. Most of that rest happens on the way to you.

    Can I skip, pause or swap a delivery?

    Yes. Skip any single roast, pause for up to three months, or switch coffee and bag size from your account, right up to the Sunday cutoff.

    How do I cancel?

    Tap Cancel plan in your account. It takes effect straight away and nothing else ships or gets charged.

    Do you sell ground coffee?

    No, whole bean only. Ground coffee goes flat within days, which would undo the point of a roast date. If you are still using pre-ground, a burr grinder is the best upgrade you can make.

    Where do you ship, and what does it cost?

    Anywhere in the contiguous US. Shipping is included in every subscription price, and most bags arrive two to three days after the roast.
